# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Recruiters and technical hiring managers who want a fast, current view of John Lindström's technical skills and contact details.

## Product Purpose

JRWL is John Lindström's personal technical profile. It stays current as his working stack changes. A visitor should understand his main tools, working approach, and availability within one short visit.

## Positioning

JRWL favors a concise, self-maintained skills record over a portfolio case study or marketing site.

## Operating Context

Visitors scan the page during candidate research on desktop or mobile. They may continue to GitHub, email John, or call him.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Keep the site as a static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript project.
- Preserve English, Finnish, and Swedish language support.
- Keep personal projects secondary.
- Do not add employer, client, or other work projects to the project list.
- Keep the technical stack current and avoid unsupported proficiency claims.

## Brand Commitments

- Keep the JRWL name and John Lindström's identity.
- Use short, plain copy that sounds written by a person.
- Use a dark, image-led orbital visual system without turning the site into a space-themed costume.
- Keep the hero compact enough that the current stack remains close to the first viewport.
- Present the technical stack once; do not repeat it as badges, highlights, or a separate table.
- Use the compact JRWL orbital wordmark in navigation; do not use cursor tracking or mouse-following decoration.
- Keep hero motion slow and image-native; do not add blinking rings, spawned overlays, or other entrance spectacle.
- Do not use maritime themes, symbols, logos, charts, or other ship-derived visual language.

## Evidence on Hand

- Dated 2026 repository evidence supports TypeScript, React, Node.js, Hono, PostgreSQL, Prisma, TanStack, Vite, Zod, Vitest, Playwright, Docker, Coolify, and recent WordPress/PHP work.
- Direct contact details and GitHub profile already exist in the site.
- Personal projects already listed on the site may remain in a reduced role.
- The site has no testimonials, employment claims, or public work-project case studies and must not invent them.

## Product Principles

- Prefer current skills over a long historical inventory.
- Let visitors scan before they read.
- Keep claims factual and compact.
- Treat projects as supporting context, not proof theatre.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve semantic structure, keyboard access, visible focus states, reduced-motion support, readable contrast, and 44-pixel mobile targets.
